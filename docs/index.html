GRR > 100% = error

Gross revenue retention above 100% is flagged as a formula error, not "great retention". The base is stated for NRR, every time.

Waterfall must reconcile

MRR_end = begin + new + expansion + reconnection − contraction − churn. A residual beyond rounding means a misclassified record — it hunts it down before reporting.

LTV sanity-capped

No 10-year lifetime fictions, no annual churn applied monthly. Logo churn and revenue churn are computed separately — different diseases, different medicine.

Audit trail always

Every headline number carries its source table, row filter, and formula. If data is insufficient, it says so instead of inventing a metric.
